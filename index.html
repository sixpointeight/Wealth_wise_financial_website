Everything you need to know about our fee structure, custodian safety, onboarding process, and fiduciary standard.

As an SEC-registered investment advisor (RIA), we are legally bound to uphold the Fiduciary Standard of Care. This means we are obligated to act solely in your best interests at all times. Unlike traditional wirehouses and broker-dealers, we do not receive commissions, sales loads, 12b-1 referral fees, or kickbacks from financial product providers. Our sole compensation comes transparently from you based on the assets we manage, aligning our success directly with yours.
Wealth Wise Financial never takes physical custody of your funds or securities.
